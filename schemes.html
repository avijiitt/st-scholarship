Comprehensive repository of centrally sponsored educational scholarships, international awards, and doctoral fellowships.

International SCH-MOTA-NOS

National Overseas Scholarship (NOS)

For higher studies abroad (Master's & Ph.D.)

Provides 100% financial assistance including tuition fee, living allowance, and return airfare for ST students studying at QS Top 500 global institutions.

Eligibility: 55%+ in Master's
Income Ceiling: ≤ ₹6.00 Lakhs/yr
Annual Awards: 20 Scholars

Research / UGC SCH-MOTA-NF

National Fellowship for ST Students (NFST)

For M.Phil & Ph.D. scholars in Indian Universities

Monthly fellowship of up to ₹38,000/month + contingency grant for pursuing regular full-time research degrees across recognized Indian universities.

Eligibility: Post-Grad + UGC NET
Income Ceiling: ≤ ₹6.00 Lakhs/yr
Annual Awards: 750 Slots
Apply via Wizard Check
Undergraduate / Degree SCH-MOTA-PMS

Post-Matric Scholarship for ST Students

Class 11, 12, UG & Professional degrees

Provides comprehensive course tuition fee reimbursement and monthly maintenance allowance directly credited through Aadhaar PFMS DBT.

Eligibility: Matriculation passed
Income Ceiling: ≤ ₹2.50 Lakhs/yr
Coverage: All State Colleges
Apply via Wizard Check
IITs / IIMs / AIIMS SCH-MOTA-TCE

Top Class Education for ST Students

Premier Notified Institutes of India

Full tuition fees, living allowances, books & stationery allowance, and computer grant for students admitted to notified premier institutes (IITs, NITs, IIMs, NLUs, AIIMS).

Eligibility: Admission to Notified Inst.
Income Ceiling: ≤ ₹6.00 Lakhs/yr
Annual Awards: 1,000 Slots
Apply via Wizard Check
School (Class 9 & 10) SCH-MOTA-PRE

Pre-Matric Scholarship for ST Students

Supporting secondary school retention

Financial support to minimize dropout rates between elementary and secondary school stages, ensuring smooth progression to higher classes.

Eligibility: Class 9th or 10th ST
Income Ceiling: ≤ ₹2.50 Lakhs/yr
Mode: State DBT Channel
Apply via Wizard Check
